# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Discuss technical decisions on
their merits, assume good faith, and make room for contributors with different
backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate intimidation, and publishing
another person's private information are not acceptable. Project maintainers
may edit or remove abusive contributions and restrict participation when needed
to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through GitHub. Reports
will be reviewed in confidence to the extent practical.
